## Hello, world!

This is the first post on the SKKUDERIA SKAI blog.

New posts go in the `_posts/` folder, named like `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`, with front matter like this post's:

```yaml
---
title: Your Post Title
date: 2026-09-01 18:30:00 +0900
categories: [Category, Subcategory]
tags: [tag1, tag2]
---
```

Then just write in Markdown below the front matter. Push to `main` and GitHub Actions will build and deploy the site automatically.
